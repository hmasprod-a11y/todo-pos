# Store demo video storyboard

Use this as the short Microsoft Store demo video plan. Record only a demo
database; never show a real shop, customer, sale or barcode.

1. **Scan** — focus the cashier barcode field and scan a demo product.
2. **Cart** — show the product, quantity, price and running net total.
3. **Pay** — open the payment view and enter cash received.
4. **Change** — keep the visible change-due value on screen.
5. **Receipt** — validate and show the printed/preview receipt.
6. **Stock** — open the stock screen and show the updated quantity.

Record one EN, one FR and one AR pass or use localized captions. Do not claim
fiscal certification, cloud backup, online payments or Store purchase features
unless those are enabled for the submitted build. Before publishing, verify that
the clip uses the current Windows artifact and that no personal/local shop data
is visible.
