# Microsoft Store listing — English

**Name:** ToDo POS  
**Subtitle:** Offline Point of Sale for Retail & Grocery

ToDo POS is an offline-first point-of-sale application for retail and grocery
shops. Scan or search products, build a receipt, accept payment, see change
due, and keep stock and cash records locally.

## Highlights
- Fast barcode search and cashier-first sale screen
- Cash, card, split and credit payment flows
- Visible last-sale reminder and receipt printing
- Products, stock, suppliers, customers and reports
- French, English and Arabic interface support
- Local backups and isolated optional demo data

Internet is not required for sale, stock, payment, returns, cash closure,
printing or supported local devices. Country profiles provide display defaults;
they do not claim fiscal compliance.
